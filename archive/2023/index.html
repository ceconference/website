2023 edition

Pathways: Building (Y)our Future

The Central European Conference 2023 focused on the prospective sectors for a career in our region, looking at the future of its markets, and discussing contemporary issues, threats, and opportunities that Central Europe offers.

CEC 2023 aimed to – and succeeded at – bringing together the Czech and Slovak communities in the UK to explore possible career pathways, and to provide a platform for discussing contemporary issues, threats, and opportunities that Central Europe faces today.

We aim to build a strong community of Czechs and Slovaks living, working, and studying abroad, and to explore the current geopolitical position of Central Europe in the world. We want to provide an overview of the latest trends and respective opportunities, encouraging students and young professionals to find their career interests while also inspiring them to give back and keep in touch with the region by trying to unveil its potential.
