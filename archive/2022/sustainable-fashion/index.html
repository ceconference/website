Sustainable Fashion

Slowing down the fast fashion cycle: how to achieve it and is it even possible?

Fast fashion has overtaken the fashion industry by its stylish products and cheap manufacturing. Few people have realized the detriment it imposes on the environment. Young liberals have undertaken the role of implementing sustainable fashion around the world. From second hand shopping to green manufacturing, sustainable fashion represents a way to help cool the planet. This debate will focus on how sustainable fashion can help the environment and what role CE plays in it. Can we make CE listen?

Speakers

Aneta Prušková

Czechia

Meet Aneta Prušková, a founder of ADVA studios and sustainability advocate with primary focus on fashion. She is best known for creating a brand that does not harm the earth as the garments are produced from sustainable and organic textile. Aneta’s two passions in life are fashion and environment and by combining these two, she successfully created a company with happy employees, ethical style and customers who enjoy both the sustainable fashion as well as the story behind each piece.

Krisztina Maróy

Hungary

Krisztina Maróy is the founder and Editor-in-Chief of the Hungarian edition of GLAMOUR. In addition to publication, she is also active in research. Her work looks into the communication practices of fashion brands and their ability to show value in the face of market inflation caused by over-production. She is Head of Ringier Axel Springer's Digital Media Campus education programme.

Barbara Jagušák

Slovakia

Barbara "Babsy" Jagušák graduated from the Middlesex University in London, with a masters degree in Strategic Marketing Management. During her studies she participated in the organisation of various events for the Slovak Olympic and Paralympic Committee. After graduation she worked in the Dynamic Relations agency, where she covered PR communication for clients in the energy, telecommunications and lifestyle industry. She is actively engaged in the Fashion Revolution Slovakia initiative and works as the PR manager of the biggest domestic fashion event Fashion LIVE!, which supports local fashion brands and designers. She created and hosts three podcasts – The New Sustainable Age, Big Storiezz by Babsy and For Mothers. She is an ambassador of sustainable fashion and supports various ecological and environmental initiatives.
