Sustainable Banking

How do banks comply with ESG goals? How can they help secure a more sustainable future?

Powered by

We, as a society, are starting to realize that money and success are not everything. Soon, we may find ourselves in a situation when our bonuses and pay raises are for nothing, as we have no drinkable water and breathable air.

But how can you react to such a phenomenon when you manage a company whose business is only about money? Is it even possible to connect and merge a traditional bank's financial goals with Sustainable Development Goals proposed by the UN? Moreover, what is your opinion as a future employee? Does an employer need to be socially and environmentally responsible to attract high skilled workers or is it just a "nice to have"?

Come to the workshop with the CEO of a bank to debate about responsible banking and other ESG activities. If you feel that climate change needs to be addressed immediately, come and find what are the barriers that keep us from being carbon neutral today, and put the environment question in the business context.

Speakers

Igor Vida

Slovakia

Igor Vida graduated from the faculty of Electrical Engineering at the Slovak University of Technology in Bratislava and went on to study General Management at Harvard Business School. He commenced his career in 1992, working for Tatra banka as a Head of Foreign exchange and Financial markets Department before transferring to the Department of Treasury and Investment banking. In 1997 he joined Tatra banka’s Board of Directors. In 2007, he became the Chairman of the Board of Directors, as well as the CEO. He joined the Raiffeisenbank Czech Republic in 2015 as Chairman of the Board of Directors and CEO. From May 2009 to April 2015, he was also the President of the Slovak Bank Association. Since 2015, he has been a member of the Presidium of the Czech Banking Association.
